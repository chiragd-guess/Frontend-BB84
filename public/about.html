How BB84 Works

BB84 // Sim is an educational simulator built to make quantum communication simple, interactive, and visual. Alice and Bob use single photons and randomly chosen bases to establish a shared secret key, while quantum mechanics makes interception detectable.

EVE: The Eavesdropper

Scroll down to see the threat model

Eve tries to intercept the photons travelling between Alice and Bob to learn the key. She cannot simply observe a quantum state without consequences: measuring a photon can disturb the state.

When Bob measures the disturbed photon, the result may disagree with Alice’s original bit. During basis comparison and QBER checking, those disturbances can appear as an elevated error rate.

⚠ If the observed error rate is above the accepted security threshold, the key should be discarded rather than trusted.

What Eve Can Try

  • Intercept and resend photons.
  • Use measurement attacks against the quantum channel.
  • Try to learn information without being detected.

Why She Fails

  • Quantum measurement can introduce detectable disturbance.
  • Alice and Bob can estimate that disturbance through QBER sampling.
  • An unacceptable QBER means the session should not produce a trusted key.
